## Chapter 1

Rain hit the tin roof of the boarding house.  
Mira (born 1986) packed a cracked pocket watch into her coat. She did not open it.  
Jonah asked what year it was. Someone downstairs said it was still early spring of 2000.  
On the table lay an unsigned letter. One line was underlined: "Do not trust the cellar stairs after dark."  
Mira left the letter where it was.

## Chapter 2

At the end of the night Mira pressed the brass cellar key into Jonah's palm.  
"Keep this. I will not carry it again," she said.  
Jonah pocketed the key.  
Later Mira wound the cracked pocket watch and the second hand jumped. She whispered, "So it still lies."  
Jonah did not hear her.

## Chapter 3

Smoke filled the hallway. Mira ran to the cellar door, pulled a brass key from her own sleeve, and unlocked it.  
"I am sixteen," Jonah told the stranger at the door. "I can vote next year."  
The cracked pocket watch fell from Mira's coat and cracked further on the stone. She stared at the lie she already knew.  
Outside, someone mentioned a map Mira had never been shown how to read. She traced the coastline without hesitation.
